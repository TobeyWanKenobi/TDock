Model | FlowerPlateV2

14:08 04/06/26

Description

A jewelry plate for my lovely girlfriend as she liked the previous BowPlate I created for her. This iterates off the lessons learnt from that model.

  • Flower pattern increases accessable surface area for things
  • Attempting doing a flower pattern underneath the base floor, instead of completely flat. Adds depth and a design.
  • Thined down base floor and walls by around 0.1cm as the BowPlate had, in my opinion, unnecessary thick floor and walls which takes up potential surface area
  • Hand traced the flower and its details from reference image.
