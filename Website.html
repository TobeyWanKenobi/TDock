Model | FlowerPlateV2

14:08 04/06/26

Description

A flower inspired jewelry plate This iterates off the lessons learnt from that model.

  • Flower pattern increases accessable surface area for things
  • Attempting doing a flower pattern underneath the base floor, instead of completely flat. Adds depth and a design.
  • Thined down base floor and walls by around 0.1cm as the BowPlate had, in my opinion, unnecessary thick floor and walls which takes up potential surface area
  • Hand traced the flower and its details from reference image.

Post Print thoughts

  • Printed well for the most part
  • Middle of the flower needs more solid support. Making it into one body with the main body.
  • Re-thicken the walls and base. Feels cheaper than the previous BowPlateV1

Event | IMechE CADathon 2026

03/03/26

Description

The Institute of Mechanical Engineers (IMechE) and Engineering without Borders (Heriot Watt Univeristy) hosted its previously annual CADathon in Glasgow for 2026. It was the first CADathon to be hosted in a few years due to COVID and funding.

Over 100 Engineering Students & Graduates attended.

I signed up and attended solo, getting assigned to a team with two other engineering students, Malcom Stuart (3rd Year, UoG) and Antoine Minyangadou (3rd Year, GCU), whom I did not know. Similar to how real engineering projects, you don't get to choose who you work with.

The goal of the CADathon was to design a system or product that solved a problem surrounding the climate. We decided on a system of modular water mist dispensers with solar pannels, able to be placed in the city for quick cooling.

With this idea we started the process of brainstorming on how it would work, going through the process an engineering team would. Creating multiple drawings, flowcharts, CAD models, and iterations of every step until presenting our work infront of a panel of judges.

This concept and our hardwork brought up all the way to the final round. Becoming a finalist in the CADathon 2026.

This was an amazing experience, esspecially in 1st year, and it opened my eye to the process of engineering. From concept to product. Further motivating me in my degree.
